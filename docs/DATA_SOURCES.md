# Data-source provenance and redistribution

Rusty Weather's software license does not relicense model data. Operators must
comply with the terms, attribution requirements, access controls, and
redistribution rules of every upstream dataset they enable.

The service maintains software capability separately from data-source policy:

- a model may be technically supported but disabled by an operator;
- credentials and provider agreements are never embedded in a store;
- each stored hour may retain bounded provider identifiers plus coarse
  pressure/surface roles and product labels; request URLs and credentials are
  never persisted;
- run identity and exact valid times remain separate from written_unix, which
  records local processing/publication time rather than upstream retrieval;
- public responses can include required source attribution without revealing
  credentials or private URLs;
- derived products inherit their hour's provider-level provenance; the current
  format does not claim per-field source lineage.

Before enabling a source in a distributed package, record the authoritative
provider page and endpoint, data license or public-domain status, attribution,
redistribution and caching restrictions, authentication requirements, update
cadence, retention expectations, and exact products and coverage used.

The sections below cover every network source that the packaged scheduler can
select. They are a release baseline, not legal advice: operators remain
responsible for reviewing changes to provider terms and for any separately
licensed data they add. The guide accompanies service release artifacts and is
distinct from THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md, which covers software and bundled static
assets.

## NOAA / NWS model data

The `noaa-nomads` lane reads operational NCEP model files and companion indexes
from [NOMADS](https://nomads.ncep.noaa.gov/). NOMADS points its users to the
[NWS Terms of Data Usage](https://www.weather.gov/disclaimer/): NWS information
is public domain unless specifically noted, may be used without charge for a
lawful purpose, must not be claimed as the user's own, must not imply NOAA/NWS
endorsement, and must not be modified and then presented as official government
material. Delivery, timeliness, completeness, and continued availability are
not guaranteed.

The `noaa-aws-public-data`, `noaa-google-public-data`, and
`noaa-microsoft-azure-public-data` lanes are transport mirrors in NOAA's
[Open Data Dissemination program](https://www.noaa.gov/information-technology/open-data-dissemination).
NODD makes NOAA open data available through Amazon Web Services, Google Cloud,
and Microsoft Azure. Using a commercial cloud mirror does not change the
underlying NOAA data status; cloud-provider service terms still govern the
operator's access to that infrastructure.

The `noaa-ncei` lane reads NOAA-originated historical model output from the
[NCEI archive](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/index.php/archive). NCEI states that
NOAA and other U.S. Federal environmental data are public domain in the United
States unless an exemption applies, while contributed non-Federal archive
holdings can retain separate CC BY or other terms. Rusty Weather enables this
lane only for the NOAA model products listed in MODEL_SUPPORT.md; operators who
point compatible tooling at other NCEI holdings must inspect that collection's
metadata and license.

For all packaged NOAA lanes:

- access is anonymous for the public endpoints currently generated by the
  registry; no provider credential is persisted;
- indexed range requests and whole-file downloads may be cached locally, but
  the scheduler cache is disabled by default and has no implied retention;
- stores and API responses preserve the resolved safe provider identifier;
- run responses include NOAA/NWS attribution, a public-domain qualification,
  a no-endorsement notice, an explicit modification notice, and the NWS
  disclaimer link;
- downstream products should credit NOAA/NWS, identify that they were
  transformed by Rusty Weather, and must not be represented as official
  NOAA/NWS products;
- cycle cadence and product scope are model-specific and are published through
  `/v1/models` and MODEL_SUPPORT.md rather than duplicated here.

## ECMWF Open Data (IFS and AIFS)

The public ECMWF lane uses the provider's
[Open Data](https://www.ecmwf.int/en/forecasts/datasets/open-data) service. The
AIFS adapter specifically reads AIFS Single v2 `oper` GRIB2 forecasts and their
line-delimited JSON `.index` companions from `data.ecmwf.int`. Its published
acquisition contract is 0.25-degree global output from 00/06/12/18 UTC cycles,
with six-hourly forecast steps from f000 through f360. Capability claims are
limited to the fields and pressure levels present in the captured provider
inventory; local inference archives are not treated as proof of public-feed
coverage.

ECMWF Open Data is published under
[CC BY 4.0 and ECMWF's Terms of Use](https://apps.ecmwf.int/datasets/licences/general/).
Redistribution and commercial use require appropriate, prominent ECMWF
attribution, a licence link, and an indication when the data has been modified.
The software licence does not replace those data terms. Deployments serving
ECMWF-derived stores must surface the required attribution to downstream
users. The safe manifest metadata identifies the resolved provider and coarse
pressure/surface product roles; it does not capture a source object URL,
retrieval timestamp, or per-field derivation lineage. `written_unix` is
processing/publication time, not a claim about when the upstream object was
retrieved. The service-facing notice must also carry the provider's required
source and disclaimer elements: identify that the service is based on ECMWF
data and products, link `www.ecmwf.int` and CC BY 4.0, and state that ECMWF
accepts no liability for errors, omissions, availability, loss, or damage
arising from use.

## User-provided and local-only sources

WRF/wrfout import is local-only. The scheduler does not discover or download a
public WRF feed, and Rusty Weather cannot determine or replace the terms of data
supplied by an operator. Operators must retain the producing institution's
license, attribution, and redistribution policy alongside the store.

Legacy local AIFS/Earth2 archive identifiers remain readable for existing
configurations, but the packaged remote scheduler accepts only the documented
ECMWF Open Data source for AIFS. The old GDEX identifier is likewise not a
packaged remote acquisition lane. No local path, source URL, bearer token, or
provider credential is emitted through the public provenance fields.
